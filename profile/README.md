# Aartsen — the international gateway for fresh fruit and vegetables

Aartsen is a family business from Breda, the Netherlands, founded in 1907 and now in its fourth generation.
We import fresh produce from growers all over the world and supply customers across the Benelux,
Northern France, Germany and Asia from our locations in Breda, Venlo, Sint-Katelijne-Waver,
Hong Kong and India.

🌐 [aartsen.com](https://www.aartsen.com)

## IT at Aartsen

Keeping fresh produce moving 24/7 takes a lot of technology behind the scenes: networks spanning
four countries, trading and logistics systems, customer-facing platforms and the automation that
holds it all together. This GitHub organisation is where our IT team builds and runs that.

Most of our work lives in private repositories. Anything we publish here is shared as-is.
